A single-player story about a quiet trade
Nightledger
Seven districts. One ledger. A city that keeps its own hours.
You run a small after-dark outfit in a wholly fictional city. Buy wholesale, prepare stock, move it across town, choose your prices and decide when to keep your head down. Buyers, other crews, weather and rumours all carry on without you. Most of that life stays behind the interface — you only see what matters tonight.
